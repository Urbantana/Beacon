You are an expert developer with deep experience in React, Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, SQLite, Tailwind CSS, and platform integration. You are working on the PalTurk project – a smart city and tourism platform.

Your task is to integrate features and content from **Bhimitkom Association** – a leading Palestinian organization for youth with disabilities – into the PalTurk platform, to enhance inclusivity and accessibility for all users.

## 🎯 Features to Implement (in parallel):

### 1. Services Directory (/services-directory)
- A page listing organizations and services available for persons with disabilities in Palestine.
- Include: Bhimitkom Association, Palestinian Independent Commission for Human Rights, AMAN Coalition, Palestinian Vision Organization, and others.
- For each organization: name, description, phone, email, location, and services offered.
- Interactive map showing organization locations.
- Arabic/English with RTL support.

### 2. Obstacle Reporting System (/report-obstacle)
- A form for reporting obstacles (broken sidewalks, unsafe paths, missing ramps, etc.).
- Fields: location (map picker), description, obstacle type, photo (optional).
- Reports are linked to the user account and earn Jawwal Points.
- Municipality dashboard to view and update report status (Pending, Under Review, In Progress, Resolved).
- Arabic/English support.

### 3. Awareness & Training Platform (/awareness)
- A page displaying courses and awareness videos on disability etiquette, sign language, white cane usage, and more.
- Content developed in collaboration with Bhimitkom Association.
- Users can enroll in courses and receive certificates upon completion.
- Arabic/English support.

### 4. Bhimitkom Store Section (/store/bhimitkom)
- A dedicated section in the store for products and services offered by youth with disabilities (embroidery, ceramics, graphic design, proofreading, photography, editing).
- Display: product name, description, price (in Points or ILS), and image.
- Users can purchase or redeem using Jawwal Points.
- Show the story behind the product and its creator.

### 5. Bhimitkom About Page (/bhimitkom)
- A page introducing Bhimitkom Association: vision, mission, team, achievements, and videos.
- Contact information: info@bhimitkom.ps, +972 59-849-5030, Facebook page.
- Display the latest important posts from the association.
- Arabic/English with RTL support.
